# CleanShots — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-19_

CleanShots is built to clean up your photo library **without compromising your
privacy**. The short version: your photos never leave your device, and we don't
collect, transmit, or sell any of your data.

## What we access

- **Your photo library**, only after you grant permission, and only to analyze
  photos for duplicates, similar shots, and best-shot recommendations. You can
  grant full or limited access, or deny access entirely.

## What happens to your photos

- **All analysis runs on your device.** Perceptual hashing, similarity detection,
  quality scoring, and any on-device AI (Apple Foundation Models) are performed
  locally on your iPhone or iPad.
- **Your photos are never uploaded.** CleanShots performs no network requests with
  your photo data. There is no server, no cloud processing, and no account.
- **We never delete photos automatically.** Cleanup always requires your review,
  and removed photos are moved to your **Recently Deleted** album (recoverable),
  never permanently erased by the app.

## What we collect

- **Nothing.** CleanShots collects no personal data, no usage analytics, and no
  identifiers. We do not track you across apps or websites.
- The app stores small preferences (such as your default scan mode) and a local
  cache of analysis results **on your device only**, to make scans faster and
  resumable. This never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

## Third parties

- CleanShots uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- On-device AI features use **Apple's Foundation Models**, which run locally. No
  prompt or photo data is sent to Apple or any other party for these features.

## Data sharing & sale

- We do not share or sell any data, because we don't collect any.

## Children's privacy

- CleanShots does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

- If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new
  "Last updated" date.
