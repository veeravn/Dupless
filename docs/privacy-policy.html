Dupless — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-20

Dupless is built to clean up your photo library without compromising your privacy. The short version: your photos never leave your device, and we don't collect, transmit, or sell any of your data.

What we access

  • Your photo library, only after you grant permission, and only to analyze photos for duplicates, similar shots, and best-shot recommendations. You can grant full or limited access, or deny access entirely.

What happens to your photos

  • All analysis runs on your device. Perceptual hashing, similarity detection, quality scoring, and any on-device AI (Apple Foundation Models) are performed locally on your iPhone or iPad.
  • Your photos are never uploaded. Dupless has no server, no cloud processing, and no account, and your photo images never leave your device.
  • We never delete photos automatically. Cleanup always requires your review, and removed photos are moved to your Recently Deleted album (recoverable), never permanently erased by the app.

Location matching (optional, off by default)

Dupless can scope an AI scan to a place you name (e.g. “my Mall of America trip”). Matching a place name to your photos requires looking up where each photo was taken, which uses Apple’s location (geocoding) service.

  • This is the only feature that sends anything off your device, it is off by default, and it runs only after you turn it on for a given scan.
  • When enabled, only photo coordinates (latitude/longitude) are sent to Apple’s geocoding service to resolve a place name — never your photos, and nothing is sent to Dupless or any other party. Apple’s handling of those requests is governed by Apple’s own privacy policy.
  • Leave it off and Dupless stays fully on-device.

What we collect

  • Nothing. Dupless collects no personal data, no usage analytics, and no identifiers. We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • The app stores small preferences (such as your default scan mode) and a local cache of analysis results on your device only, to make scans faster and resumable. This never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Third parties

  • Dupless uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • On-device AI features use Apple's Foundation Models, which run locally. No prompt or photo data is sent to Apple or any other party for these features.

Data sharing & sale

We do not share or sell any data, because we don't collect any.

Children's privacy

Dupless does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
